# 5–7 Minute Demo
0:00–0:30 Explain procurement pain point.
0:30–1:00 Introduce ProcureAI and decision-support architecture.
1:00–2:00 Show dashboard, KPIs and 10-vendor data.
2:00–3:00 Change weights and demonstrate ranking.
3:00–4:00 Show cost/quality, delivery and risk analytics.
4:00–5:00 Ask Gemini why the top vendor ranks first and request due-diligence considerations.
5:00–6:00 Run Quality First or Cost Optimization what-if strategy.
6:00–6:30 Load invalid data and show validation.
6:30–7:00 Close with business value, responsible AI and CSV→SQL/cloud/ERP roadmap.
